# Clarifications and bounded design decisions

20 October 2026. These decisions resolve implementation ambiguity conservatively; they are not evidence of completed code. Changes require an updated rationale and affected tests.

1. **Direct editing means live draft editing.** Preserve accepted-scene Apply/export and snapshot isolation. Gesture history is explicitly draft-scoped and bounded, distinct from accepted-scene Undo/Redo. No silent auto-commit is inferred.
2. **Startup versus playback.** The requested preview remains visible statically at startup. Direct interaction never starts pulse/fade clocks; Hide remains persistent until explicit resume.
3. **All shapes means families.** An exhaustive geometric inventory is impossible. Implement finite parameterized families with validated ranges and extension contracts; do not accept arbitrary executable shape definitions.
4. **Schema expansion is explicit.** P1 can use current scene-v3 coordinates. P2 uses scene-v4 and sequence-v3, strict old-version validation, and migration tests. Sequence capture must preserve canvas metadata and reject mismatched snapshot canvases; until the corresponding sequence contract is implemented, capture of unsupported new-format scenes must fail explicitly rather than drop fields. Exact field names are frozen during IF-048 before dependent implementation, not guessed in UI code.
5. **Authored canvas is opt-in.** Preserve legacy viewport-relative semantics. New fixed-ratio scenes use explicit content-rectangle mapping and letterboxing, with a preview of conversion. The new-canvas UI offers common ratios, with square as the default for explicitly created fixed-ratio scenes; supported ratios and their exact bounds are frozen with IF-048. Existing starter/legacy scenes are not silently converted. No silent crop/stretch or physical calibration claim.
6. **Counts and resource caps stay bounded.** Preserve 12 items and existing canvas allocation limits until measured evidence and review justify a change. New path/image budgets must have boundary tests and a documented rationale; they are not safety thresholds.
7. **Initial photo mode is local abstraction.** No third-party model/API, remote URL input or original-image export. Filled approximation waits for compatible geometry. A sparse sketch may be benchmarked earlier but is not promised as photo recreation.
8. **Journal persistence is not inferred.** Initial optional journal is session memory with explicit separate export and clear loss-on-close notice. No localStorage/IndexedDB or hidden autosave. Durable storage needs a later explicit decision.
9. **Research goal is not assumed.** Initial software supports usability/preference/tolerability reflection, not demonstrated imagery change. A future study must choose its primary question, outcomes and meaningful effect before collecting reports.
10. **Submissions stay disabled.** Implementing a contract/mock does not enable collection. Operator, hosting/security, jurisdictions, ethics, instrument rights, retention and statistical design are external prerequisites, not TODOs that code can satisfy.
11. **Verification wording is scoped.** Automated Linux Chrome, emulated mobile, inspected screenshots, physical devices, assistive technology, qualified content review and participant research have separate evidence states. “Fully tested” cannot mean all browsers/devices or clinical efficacy without evidence.
12. **Single status source.** Only [project-queue.json](../../project-queue.json) records live completion. [tasks.md](tasks.md) is a fixed execution and acceptance map, not a duplicate checkbox board.

## Fourth increment: session locks and precise arrangement

This is the authorized implementation contract for IF-050, not a claim that the new controls or tests are complete. Implementation, exact-source tests and independent review remain pending. It does not expand the file schema, display capabilities, persistence or research authority.

13. **A lock protects the stored item, not the entire picture.** Keep at most 12 defensive canonical item snapshots keyed by item ID in session memory. Protect shape, geometry, position, color, effects and the item's own timing/pulse fields. Global background, canvas ratio, shared pulse period and other items can still change the composite. A locked item remains selectable; a topmost locked hit must not click through or start a drag. Identify locks in text.
14. **Locking cannot accept invalid input or finish an old gesture.** Cancel/roll back active dragging and placement before locking, unlocking or changing snap. Validate before storing a lock; invalidate stale imports and asynchronous edit results. Every candidate write preflights the complete locked records before state, selection or history changes. Disabled controls alone are not enforcement. Layer movement of the locked selection is prohibited; duplication is allowed only as a new unlocked independent copy, leaving its source unchanged.
15. **History and unlocking remain explicit.** Apply and Add/Replace snapshot capture retain locks. Accepted-scene Undo/Redo are unavailable while any lock exists, with an explanation and reachable **Unlock all**. Draft move Undo/Redo and Discard may proceed only when their concrete candidate leaves every locked record unchanged; rejection must not consume history. Hide, Stop, End, Escape, exports and unlocking are never blocked by locks.
16. **Only successful scene replacement clears locks.** New fixed-ratio scene, explicit legacy conversion, either preset selector, gallery Use, scene import, Load step into scene editor, image Use and Undo image draft replacement clear locks only after successful validation/replacement, with a visible notice. Rejected, canceled, failed or stale replacements retain the prior scene and locks. Importing a sequence alone retains editor locks; successfully loading a step into the scene clears them. Generic field refresh is not a replacement boundary.
17. **Snap is session-only and limited to drag/placement.** Off initially; choices are Off, 1, 5 and 10 normalized content units. Preserve the drag grab offset and use the actual content rectangle. Choose the nearest feasible grid index inside the conservative inset, rather than clamping to an off-grid edge. Reject letterbox taps. Changing snap alone does not move an item, add display marks, reveal output or start timing. Typed positions, button/keyboard nudges and alignment stay exact and unsnapped, including Shift+arrow's 0.1-unit movement.
18. **Centering is a bounded draft move.** Center horizontally sets X to 50, vertically sets Y to 50, and both sets both axes, for the selected unlocked item. Validate atomically; a no-op creates no history and a successful change creates one existing bounded draft-move operation, not Apply. Hidden output stays hidden; an already active valid static preview may update.
19. **No implied distribution or grid.** Equal-spacing distribution, visible grid/guides, grouping and persistent settings remain deferred. Locks and snap are absent from scene/sequence downloads, and toggling them with no active gesture must not change canonical scene identity or add pixels. No scene schema version bump or new clock is authorized.

## Decisions awaiting measured implementation or qualified authority

- Image byte/pixel/memory/iteration limits are implemented engineering bounds; representative performance, held-out natural-image usefulness and correction-effort acceptance remain open under IF-051/IF-052.
- Session-lock mutation/replacement coverage and snap/centering integration: IF-050 requires model, UI, native and inspected-render evidence before acceptance.
- Public research operator, approved protocol, retention/withdrawal and consent text: IF-057 external gate; no real submission while unresolved.
- Physical-device and assistive-technology coverage: IF-059; record unavailable tests as NOT RUN, never substitute mocks.
