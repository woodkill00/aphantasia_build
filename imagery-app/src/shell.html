Use comfortable screen brightness and ordinary room lighting. Don’t increase brightness or keep staring to make an image persist. Static patterns can still cause symptoms.

    
Read the viewing guidance
      
If you have photosensitive epilepsy, or visual patterns trigger seizures, migraine, or other symptoms, get advice from your clinician before using this activity.

      
Stop if you experience headache, dizziness, nausea, eye pain, or a visual disturbance. Seek medical advice for new, persistent, or recurring visual symptoms. Sudden vision loss or severe eye pain needs urgent medical care.
